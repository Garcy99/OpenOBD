OpenOBD turns your phone and an ELM327-compatible OBD-II adapter (Bluetooth, Bluetooth LE or Wi-Fi) into a complete car diagnostic tool. It's free and open source, with no ads, subscriptions or account.

DIAGNOSTICS
• Read confirmed, pending and permanent trouble codes from every ECU, and clear them (check engine light)
• Offline database of 1,300+ generic codes, with causes, severity and repair tips for common ones
• Freeze frame, emission readiness (smog check) and Mode 06 monitor tests, including misfire counts per cylinder
• Vehicle information: VIN decoder, calibration IDs, CVN, ECU names

BRAND FEATURES
• Brand detected automatically from the VIN
• Scan ABS, airbag, body, cluster, steering, climate and EV battery modules (UDS / KWP2000 on CAN)
• Known module addresses for Ford/Lincoln, Mazda, VW/Audi/Škoda/SEAT, Hyundai/Kia, Toyota/Lexus, Nissan, Renault and GM, plus discovery for any brand
• Module freeze frames, failure types and "what to check" hints
• Support for HS/MS-CAN switch adapters (Ford/Mazda)
• Custom PIDs with Torque CSV import

LIVE DATA
• Every standard OBD-II PID (200+ sensors), plus calculated fuel economy, boost, power and torque
• Custom gauge dashboard, real-time charts, head-up display mode and alerts
• CSV data logging in the background
• Fast polling with multi-PID requests and OBDLink/STN fast mode

GARAGE
• Several cars, each with its own settings and history
• Maintenance reminders by distance and time
• Fuel log with real consumption and costs
• Trip history with GPS map
• Home-screen widget

Try it without a car: Demo mode simulates a vehicle.

OpenOBD only reads data and clears trouble codes; it never writes coding or adaptations. Don't use the app while driving. Some features depend on your adapter and vehicle.
