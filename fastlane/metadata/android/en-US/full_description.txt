OpenOBD turns your phone, tablet or car head unit and an ELM327-compatible OBD-II adapter (Bluetooth, Bluetooth LE, Wi-Fi or USB) into a complete car diagnostic tool. Free and open source: no ads, no subscriptions, no account, and your data stays on your phone.

DIAGNOSE
• Read and clear trouble codes in the engine and in every module: ABS, airbag, body, cluster, gearbox, climate, EV battery
• Codes explained: "can I keep driving?", likely causes ranked with your car's own data, what to check next
• 1,300+ code descriptions offline, plus manufacturer codes for Ford, Toyota, Honda, Nissan, GM, Chrysler and Hyundai / Kia
• Used car & pre-inspection check with a PDF report: hidden faults, codes cleared recently, readiness, VIN in every module
• Freeze frame, emission readiness, Mode 06 tests with misfires per cylinder, battery & starting test, health trends
• Optional AI assistant with your own Claude API key, also to identify a warning light from a photo

LIVE DATA
• 200+ sensors, real fuel consumption (also for diesels), boost, power and accelerator
• Gauges your way: 7 themes, needle, arc, bar or number, colours, ranges and warnings for every gauge
• Charts, HUD, floating gauges over navigation apps, voice alerts, background CSV logging
• Trip computer, 0-100 km/h timer and eco-driving score

MANUFACTURER DATA AND CODINGS
• Community data from OBDb for almost every brand: battery health, DPF soot and regenerations, tyres, gearbox…
• Particulate filter screen with regeneration conditions and the manufacturer's DPF values
• Codings & adaptations from community packs or forum lists (UDS, KWP2000, GMLAN, BMW / MINI gateway). Every change is backed up first, checked afterwards, restored automatically if it fails and can be undone. Dangerous changes explain the risk and ask twice
• Identifier explorer, custom PIDs with Torque CSV import, shareable PID packs

GARAGE
• Several cars, recognised by VIN
• Find your car in the official EU catalog (EEA), by licence plate (NL, UK) or by VIN (US)
• Maintenance and inspection reminders, fuel log with real consumption
• Trips on a map: tap any point to see speed, rpm and consumption there
• Home-screen widget, backup and restore

WORKS WITH
• Every OBD-II car (EU petrol 2001+, diesel 2004+, US 1996+), all OBD-II protocols
• Known module addresses for Ford, Mazda, VW group, Hyundai / Kia, Toyota / Lexus, Nissan, Renault, GM, Opel / Vauxhall and BMW / MINI; discovery for every other brand
• Cheap ELM327 clones, OBDLink / STN (fast mode) and HS/MS-CAN switch adapters
• 11 languages; light, dark and AMOLED themes; phones, tablets and car head units

Try it without a car: Demo mode simulates a vehicle.

Don't use the app while driving. Codings are only written when you choose them, with a backup first; protected modules are never unlocked. Some features depend on your adapter and vehicle.
